# research-tools

Choose the right tool for investigation:

- **Sourcegraph**: finding patterns, implementations, and precedent in the organization's codebase.
- **Web search**: external APIs, libraries, standards (RFCs, AEPs), vendor docs, and anything not in the org's code.
- **Notion MCP**: internal docs, decision logs, runbooks, and scratchpads.

If the first tool yields nothing after 2 attempts, switch to a different tool rather than repeating the same search.
